Functionality statement

I confirm that the DVS PSS equipment detailed in this report has been installed, tested, and verified as fully operational in accordance with Transport for London's DVS PSS requirements.

MOIS — Detects vulnerable road users in the vehicle's path when moving off; provides an audible and/or visual warning to the driver.

BSIS — Detects vulnerable road users in the nearside blind spot during left turns and low-speed manoeuvres; provides an audible and/or visual warning to the driver.

The equipment has been installed by a Motacam-certified technician and is confirmed to be functioning correctly at the time and date stated on this report. This report serves as the installation certificate for DVS PSS compliance purposes.

TfL DVS PSS requirements apply to HGVs operating in Greater London under the HGV Safety Permit Scheme · tfl.gov.uk/dvs
